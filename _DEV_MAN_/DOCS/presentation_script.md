## Solution Overview (1 minute)

Our solution works in four simple steps:

1. **Document Capture**: The system accepts purchase orders in any format - PDF, scanned image, or even email text
2. **AI Extraction**: Advanced AI extracts all relevant data - vendor information, line items, quantities, prices, and terms
3. **Smart Validation**: The system validates the extracted data, checks calculations, and flags any issues
4. **QuickBooks Integration**: All data is automatically populated in QuickBooks, creating or updating vendors, items, and purchase orders
